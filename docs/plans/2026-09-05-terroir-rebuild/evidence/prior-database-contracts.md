## Security and transaction invariants

1. **Business is the operational isolation boundary; venue narrows authority.** Carry `business_id` on sensitive operational rows and validate venue/lot/supplier ownership through composite foreign keys or equivalent database constraints. A parent group's organizational link is not a data grant. Quantities, costs, supplier terms, export rights and role administration need separate explicit permissions. Group reports aggregate only businesses the actor can read; hidden businesses must not leak through totals.

2. **Validate at commit time.** Recheck membership, venue grants and request ownership on every mutation, including queued work and reconnecting drafts. RLS is the backstop; service-role jobs require narrowly scoped commands and explicit business/venue filtering. Existing stock/closeout ownership fixes are useful precedents: `0136_wine_ownership_on_write_policies.sql:68-105`. Test forged references, role removal, stale sessions and indirect storage/export access.

3. **Quantity, unit and ownership are explicit.** Use integer bottle counts for sealed units and a declared exact volume unit for open stock; a serving cannot silently become one bottle. Product volume and packaging conversions are versioned inputs. Owner business differs from ordering actor and custody location. Launch forbids movements between different owning businesses. No ownership-transfer, resale or accounting rule is inferred for the later central-stock expansion.

4. **Commit one operational fact atomically.** Receiving posts receipt acceptance, lot allocations, movements, balances and audit/outbox records together. Dispatch moves stock from venue availability into transit; receipt moves the accepted amount out of transit. Partial receipts, damage, shortages, cancellation and return need explicit states. They never silently recreate the original venue balance. Lock affected stock accounts/lots in consistent order; reject insufficient available stock or open a reconciliation case under a defined exception policy.

5. **Idempotency spans effects.** Unique scoped request keys prevent duplicate receiving, dispatch, receipt and stock corrections. Reversals reference the original movement, with quantity bounds enforced under locks. Audit/event records cannot disappear because someone deletes a wine or venue; archive referenced subjects. Existing stock events cascade with wine deletion (`0063_stock_adjustments.sql:10-20`), so their present “immutable” grant does not satisfy this proposed retention contract.

6. **Counts are observations.** Record count cutoff/time and expected stock version; reconcile movements occurring during the count. A manager explicitly approves a variance movement. Offline counts, photographs and notes remain labeled pending until authorization and conflicts are checked. Launch does not silently queue offline receiving, transfers or consumption. Cache access, expiration and cleanup after revocation require a device policy; never promise remote revocation can erase an offline device immediately.

7. **Toast facts and stock decisions remain distinguishable.** Scope external IDs by connection and provider object identity; preserve event/revision ordering and mapping version. Commit accepted deduction, applied-revision marker and checkpoint/outbox effects atomically. Unknown mappings, unsupported modifiers, insufficient stock or conflicting revisions create visible cases. A financial refund does not automatically mean a physical bottle returned: record financial status separately and apply the approved restock disposition. Handle voids, edits, replays and late events without double depletion. Provider delivery and entitlement details still need verification; do not assume exactly-once webhooks or all historical data are available.

   **Proposed manual/Toast reconciliation contract:** designate a consumption-recording mode per venue and effective interval. Link a POS sale to an already-recorded manual pour only through a validated reference or reviewed match; a matched sale adds sales evidence without another stock deduction. Unmatched or ambiguous overlap remains a case, never a guessed automatic match. Define how shadow-mode history crosses into automated deduction before enabling it. Manual pour logging must not remain an independent unconditional stock writer alongside Toast.

8. **Money retains its unit.** Preserve source cost and currency; never average incompatible currencies. Reporting conversion requires a chosen currency and dated conversion basis, or an unknown result. Current pricing recompute drops currency and sums costs (`src/lib/pricing-recommendations/recompute.ts:108`, `:178-190`). Purchasing allocations and receipt discrepancies must not imply a financial general ledger.

9. **Public publication is a separate privilege.** Geography retains geometry type, granularity, source, attribution, permitted use and version; a regional location is not a vineyard coordinate. Content records the evidence set, contributor, automated editorial policy version, exceptions, sampling decisions and rollback target. Automated review may publish qualifying prose, per owner decision; raw contributor submissions never directly mutate public facts. Keep independently sourced ratings separate from producer contributions. Excluding private fields at rendering time alone is insufficient: enforce safe public projections, storage access and search-index boundaries.

10. **Identity correction never rewrites stock history implicitly.** Proposed global merge commands retain original IDs, aliases, source assertions, decision evidence and redirects; invalidating a merge is traceable. Merging public products must not combine different vintages/formats, erase lot costs, collapse private wine overrides or retrospectively change Toast mapping meaning. Version mapping and search projections, then recompute derived displays under each tenant's authority. Preserve original event identity snapshots. Existing separate canonical and tenant merges (`supabase/migrations/0100_wine_identity_merge.sql`) are reuse candidates, not proof that this expanded contract is met.

## Later contracts to reserve in the matrix

- **Collector:** proposed `personal_cellars`, scoped memberships, holdings/locations, purchase plans, private tasting notes and receipts. Default private; no business-admin access. Proposed `note_copy_grants` and copied-note lineage implement business-authorized selected copies; authorship alone grants no export. Copy content, revocation semantics and retention still need decisions. The advisory guide needs explicit `context_consents`, scoped retrieval, source references and usage limits; no action privileges.
- **Supplier and consumer coordination:** proposed `supplier_organizations`, verified representations, `trading_relationships`, account-specific catalogs/offers, quote revisions and coordinated orders linked to buyer purchasing. Sellers retain payment/fulfillment responsibility. External listings carry link-out status and observed freshness; Terroir cannot invent order status. Consumer merchant listings are separate from public global learning.
- **Central stock expansion:** represent owning business, custody and ordering responsibility separately now; define later ownership changes, distribution pricing, invoicing, allocation and reversal rules from interviews/testing. No generic launch “transfer” endpoint may bypass the same-business restriction. No promise that these columns eliminate future migrations.
- **Community:** proposed invited groups and content-audience grants first; opt-in public profiles, follows/posts and moderation later. Work-note copy permission, cellar invitation and public-post permission are different grants. Verified professional affiliation remains a separate assertion. Community opinion never overwrites reference facts automatically.
- **Subscriptions:** separate payer/entitlement records from content authority and ranking. Sponsorship, if later implemented, uses distinct labeled placement records and cannot influence organic ranking or guide recommendations. Exact billing units and prices remain undecided.

## Contracts that must be settled before implementation

The highest-risk gaps are the permission matrix, quantity/lot allocation rules, transfer discrepancy state machine, effective-time Toast mapping and restock policy, public-source rights/coverage targets, and editorial exception/rollback ownership. Set measurable pilot acceptance cases for each. Test simultaneous pours and transfers, partial receipt twice, counts during sales, old Toast revisions, member removal before offline sync, and denied cross-business references. Test retrieval/cache/export surfaces as well as row reads.

The five-week target and USD800 coding-subscription plus USD100 miscellaneous monthly envelope are constraints, not evidence of feasibility. Preserve the complete-release scope while estimating these contracts, source/map costs and owner/Rohan exception capacity. An isolated environment can support early production testing without deleting existing data; require separate production/staging identity checks and migration/restore evidence. Existing provider access, data rights, real staff trials, concurrency performance and financial correctness remain verification gates. Choose rebuild versus iteration only after the feature matrix and its acceptance criteria are complete.
