## FINDINGS

**1. [P1] Capacity model multiplies WIP against single-builder productive hours; the adopted 9-PR plan is not supported by its own numbers.**
Evidence: 03-delivery.md §Capacity calculation — "Eleven planned days ×8 productive hours/day=88 hours"; "At 2PR WIP and effective 12/16/24 productive hours per PR, upper bounds are 14/11/7 merges." 06-capacity.md §Measured capacity — "one builder on Mini is the capacity baseline"; MacBook "Unavailable for committed capacity."
Defect: 88 h is a *single-stream productive-hour* budget. Multiplying by WIP=2 double-counts a builder who cannot author two PRs simultaneously. Divided correctly, 88 ÷ 12–24 h = **3.7–7.3 PRs**, below the adopted 9. Independently, the track table's own total (60–92 h) implies 6.7–10.2 h/PR across 9 PRs — below the 12/16/24 band used in the same section. And the upper estimate (92 h) exceeds the 88 h budget before any contingency.
Consequence: the Conditional GO is issued against a capacity figure roughly 2× the supportable one; S predictably overruns into buffer by Day 7–8, and the deadline verdict is not honest as stated.
Fix: restate Little's law with elapsed hours in the numerator or drop the WIP multiplier; publish one unit (productive hours). Re-derive the PR count from the corrected bound and reduce S (manual-receipt-only fallback is already specified in 03-delivery.md §S).
Closure: revised §Capacity where PR count ≤ (available productive hours ÷ low-end per-PR hours), and total track hours ≤ available hours at the *high* estimate, with staffing hours confirmed by the owner.

**2. [P1] The restore rehearsal sits on Day 11 of the critical path and depends on infrastructure with explicitly unbounded lead time and no fallback.**
Evidence: 03-delivery.md §Days and waves, Day 11 "W5 protected restore rehearsal … Gate2 readiness"; 03-delivery.md §Estimated tracks "External approval lead time is unbounded until verified"; 02-architecture-data.md §Environments — recovery environment requires "Owner-approved destination and access"; 01-evidence.md conflict 2 — the only drill is a historical Aug 22 PASS.
Consequence: if owner provisioning of the recovery environment slips past ~Day 8, Gate 2 cannot be met inside 14 days, and buffer Days 12–14 (Day 14 also holds the owner Gate 2 decision) cannot absorb it. Day-3 fallbacks in 06-capacity.md cover AWS/MacBook/Blacksmith/queue but **not** the recovery environment.
Fix: move recovery-environment provisioning and a first restore attempt to Wave 0/Day 1–3 as a hard prerequisite; add an explicit Day-3 fallback (rehearsal unavailable → S ships as synthetic demonstration, deadline NO-GO) mirroring the existing Day-3 rule.
Closure: schedule shows rehearsal start ≤ Day 5 with a named owner-provisioning date, plus a written fallback branch.

**3. [P1] Track C authors, integrates, holds LAND-GO and owns the state ledger; no non-author findings-ledger owner is named for C- or Q-authored slices.**
Evidence: 04-ownership.md — C owns contracts *and* "repository state ledger"; PRs 1,2,4,8,9 are authored by C or Q. 05-ci-tests.md — "One integrator locks landing… LAND-GO requires… explicit integration authority." 07-protocol.md — "Non-author Q/ledger steward consolidates findings"; "Authors may respond with evidence but may not close their own findings."
Consequence: for the 5 of 9 PRs authored by C or Q, the findings steward is the author, and C effectively self-lands. This breaks the governing requirement's closure-authority rule at exactly the contract/schema boundary where cross-tenant defects would appear.
Fix: name a steward per authoring track (e.g. D stewards C/Q slices, Q stewards D/R/U/O), and separate LAND-GO authority from authorship — the integrator may not land a PR they authored.
Closure: 07-protocol.md table mapping every planned PR → author, steward, lander, with no cell repeating the author.

**4. [P1] Real pilot data is admitted to the candidate environment with no classification, region/account constraint, backup, retention or deletion plan.**
Evidence: 02-architecture-data.md §Environments — candidate is "synthetic or demonstrably anonymized… new pilot accounts only after Gate2. Real pilot data subsequently treated as production-sensitive"; §Retention covers only *existing* environments. START-HERE §Decision options — S includes "reviewed manual/invoice receipt" for a named business/venue.
Consequence: real invoices, supplier terms and staff identities land in a project provisioned as a test environment, with no stated backup, no retention clock, no deletion path if the pilot is abandoned, and no approved account/region — a data-handling exposure the plan otherwise guards carefully for production.
Fix: extend §Environments and §Retention to the pilot: named account/region, encrypted backup, retention window, deletion-on-abandon procedure, and Gate 2 checklist rows for each.
Closure: Gate 2 checklist contains pilot-data backup/retention/deletion items with a named owner.
